# SuikaGame
This is our game, we recreated Suika Game, but with our own changes, like our version


To play you have to click on the box, using your mouse to move it to rigth or left to where you want the fruit to fall.

Keep merging  the fruits to make bigger ones, that gives different amounts of points depending on each fruit.


The next fruit to appear will appear on your left, so you can make better decitions on where to place the fruits.

If you reach the top of the box for more than 2 seconds, the game ends.

Try to get the watermelon/sandia. :D
